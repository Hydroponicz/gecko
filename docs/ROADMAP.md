1. ⏳ **Player** (built, awaiting test) — third-person movement + camera, keyboard/mouse + PS5 DualSense from day one.
2. **Factory gray box** — empty room, placement grid, the four machines, Stash, hand-carrying.
3. **Money & supplies** — dirty cash HUD, buying supplies.
4. **Workers & routes**
5. **Vehicles & delivery** — gray-box street, drivable car, buyer.
6. **Robberies & street dealing** — starting-money sources.
7. **Police & heat**

After the core loop works: combat, laundering, territory, crew hierarchy, more properties.
